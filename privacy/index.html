Privacy Policy

Last updated October 21, 2026

GradeMyInsurance.com is operated by Dixon Insurance Agency, a licensed insurance agency in South Carolina and North Carolina. This policy explains what we collect through this website and how we use it.

What we collect

When you request a quote, we collect your name, phone number, email address, ZIP code, your answers to the questions on the form (such as whether you own or rent, your role, or your school or district), the coverage you want quoted, and your renewal timing. Like most websites, we also collect basic usage data through cookies and similar tools, including the Meta Pixel.

How we use it

We use your information to contact you about your quote, prepare a coverage comparison, and provide insurance service you request. We use usage data to measure and improve our advertising.

Calls and texts

If you check the consent box, we may call or text you at the number you provided about your quote, including by automated means. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Reply STOP to any text to opt out, or ask us by phone or email. Message and data rates may apply.

Who we share it with

We share your information with insurance companies only to obtain quotes you request, and with service providers that help us run this site and our business, such as our website host, email, spreadsheet, and automation tools. We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies and advertising

We use the Meta Pixel to understand which ads lead to quote requests. You can limit ad tracking in your Facebook and browser settings.

How long we keep it

We keep your information as long as needed to serve you and to meet legal and record-keeping requirements.

Your choices

To update or delete your information, or to stop hearing from us, contact us at quote@grademyinsurance.com or (864) 707-1131.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Insurance products are underwritten by the insurance companies we represent. Coverage, discounts, and savings vary by company, state, and individual circumstances. A review does not guarantee lower rates.
